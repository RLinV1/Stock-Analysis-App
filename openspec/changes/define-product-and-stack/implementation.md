## Update 2026-09-20 — volatility-instrument audit complete

**Created:** `docs/FINDING-VIX-INSTRUMENT.md`.

The audit went beyond literature review and empirically reconstructed the paper's baselines from
Cboe VIX history and Yahoo adjusted closes over the paper's exact test window. Finding: strong
evidence that the authors treated the raw VIX index level as a tradable price series. Three
near-exact volatility matches against the paper's Table 1 under the index; the tradable
alternative is off by 2-2.5x with the wrong sign on every expected return. Corroborated by the
temporal impossibility of any VIX ETP existing in 2006, and by zero occurrences of futures,
contango, roll, or ETN in the paper text.

Resolution adopted: three-tier reporting. Tier 1 fidelity run on the raw index, labelled not
investable. Tier 2 tradable run on VXX backfilled by VIXY before 2018-01-25 (same index, measured
correlation 0.987, 0.35pp annualized tracking gap). Tier 3 three-asset diagnostic isolating
whether the volatility sleeve contributes anything at all.

`tasks.md` item 2.1 with two new follow-ups.
